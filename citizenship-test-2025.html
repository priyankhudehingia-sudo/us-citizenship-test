What is the 2025 U.S. Citizenship Test?

The 2025 civics test uses an official bank of 128 civics questions and answers. During the naturalization interview, the USCIS officer asks up to 20 questions, and an applicant must answer at least 12 correctly to pass this version of the civics test.

Answers involving elected or appointed officials can change. USCIS instructs applicants to use the name of the official serving at the time of the naturalization interview.

How to study

  1. Learn the official 128 civics questions.
  2. Use randomized practice to avoid memorizing question order.
  3. Take repeated 20-question mock tests and aim for at least 12 correct.
  4. Review questions you miss and repeat them until you are comfortable answering aloud.
  5. Check USCIS for current answers to questions about officials, your state, or your district.

Special 65/20 consideration

USCIS states that applicants who are 65 or older and have been a lawful permanent resident for 20 or more years may study the 20 specially marked questions and receive a 10-question version of the civics test, with 6 correct required to pass.

Eligibility and test rules can change. Use the official USCIS guidance for your situation.
