1. What is the form of government of the United States?

Answer: Republic

2. What is the supreme law of the land?

Answer: The Constitution

3. Name one thing the U.S. Constitution does.

Answer: Forms the government

4. The U.S. Constitution starts with the words "We the People." What does "We the People" mean?

Answer: Self-government

5. How are changes made to the U.S. Constitution?

Answer: Amendments

6. What does the Bill of Rights protect?

Answer: The basic rights of Americans

7. How many amendments does the U.S. Constitution have?

Answer: Twenty-seven (27)

8. Why is the Declaration of Independence important?

Answer: It says America is free from British control

9. What founding document said the American colonies were free from Britain?

Answer: Declaration of Independence

10. Name two important ideas from the Declaration of Independence and the U.S. Constitution.

Answer: Equality

11. The words "Life, Liberty, and the pursuit of Happiness" are in what founding document?

Answer: Declaration of Independence

12. What is the economic system of the United States?

Answer: Capitalism

Answer: Everyone must follow the law

14. Many documents influenced the U.S. Constitution. Name one.

Answer: Declaration of Independence

15. There are three branches of government. Why?

Answer: Checks and balances

16. Name the three branches of government.

Answer: Legislative, executive, and judicial

17. The President of the United States is in charge of which branch of government?

Answer: Executive branch

Answer: U.S. Congress

Answer: The Senate and House of Representatives

Answer: Writes laws

Answer: Two (2) years

Answer: Citizens from their congressional district
